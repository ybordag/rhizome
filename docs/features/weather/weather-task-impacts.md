# Weather Task Impacts

Weather task impact analysis decides whether forecast conditions materially
change the safety, timing, or urgency of planned work.

## Current Behavior

Derives impacts such as frost, heat, heavy rain, storms, and good planting
windows, then identifies materially affected tasks.

## Impact Types

- Frost or freeze risk.
- Heat stress and watering urgency.
- Heavy rain, saturated soil, and poor transplant windows.
- Wind or storm risk for support, protection, or delay work.
- Favorable planting, hardening-off, or outdoor work windows.

## Contract Notes

- Impact matching should be based on structured task attributes, dates, and
  linked garden subjects where possible.
- Severity should explain why a task is urgent, delayed, or unchanged.
- Advisory impacts can inform triage without drafting a task mutation.
- Mutating task schedules requires a weather change set and approval unless a
  documented monitor policy explicitly auto-applies a narrow critical case.
